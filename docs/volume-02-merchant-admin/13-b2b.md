# 2.13 B2B

## Purpose
Wholesale commerce layer on top of the standard DTC catalog: company accounts with multiple buyers, negotiated price lists, quantity breaks, and net-payment terms — letting one store serve both retail and wholesale customers.

## Navigation
Sidebar entry surfaces under `Customers → Company Accounts` and a dedicated `B2B` settings area under `Settings`. Sub-tabs: Company Accounts, Price Lists, Payment Terms, Quote Requests.

## User roles
Owner/Manager: full access. Finance: manage payment terms/credit limits, view price lists. Marketing: manage price lists (promotional wholesale pricing), no credit-limit access. Sales (Staff subtype): manage assigned company accounts and quotes, no price-list creation.

## Permissions
`b2b.view`, `b2b.manage_companies`, `b2b.manage_price_lists`, `b2b.manage_payment_terms`, `b2b.approve_credit_limit`.

## Fields
Company: name, tax ID/VAT, billing/shipping addresses, assigned price list, payment terms (Net 30/60/90, prepay), credit limit, status (Active/On hold). Buyer (contact within a company): name, email, role (Admin/Buyer — company-scoped, distinct from store staff roles), order approval requirement (yes/no, and threshold).
Price list: name, assigned companies, product/variant overrides, quantity-break tiers (e.g., 10+ units = $X, 50+ = $Y).

## Buttons
Create company, Invite buyer, Assign price list, Set credit limit, Place order on behalf of, Create quote, Convert quote to order, Approve/reject pending order (buyer-approval workflow).

## Tables
Companies list: name, price list, payment terms, credit limit, balance/outstanding, status. Company detail → buyers table, order history table. Price list detail → tiered pricing table per product.

## Filters
Payment terms type, status, credit utilization (near-limit), assigned sales rep.

## Search
Company name, buyer email, quote number.

## Bulk actions
Bulk assign price list to companies, bulk payment-terms update, bulk price-list CSV import.

## Workflows
1. Onboard company → create record → assign price list + payment terms + credit limit → invite primary buyer → buyer invites additional buyers within their company (self-service, capped by company admin settings).
2. Buyer shops at wholesale prices (auto-applied based on logged-in company) → for orders requiring approval (above a threshold or by policy), order enters "Pending approval" → company Admin buyer or merchant staff approves → order proceeds to standard fulfillment (Vol 2.2).
3. Net-terms order: order placed without immediate payment capture → invoice generated with due date per terms → payment collected later (manually or via automated dunning) → ties into Finance (Vol 2.8) as an accounts-receivable entry rather than an immediate settled transaction.
4. Quote request: buyer requests custom pricing/quantity → Sales staff responds with a quote → buyer accepts → quote converts to an order at quoted terms.

## Business rules
- Wholesale pricing only applies to authenticated buyers of an Active company account; anonymous/retail visitors always see standard/Market pricing (Vol 2.11).
- Orders placed against net terms cannot exceed the company's available credit (limit − outstanding balance) unless explicitly overridden by Finance/Owner.
- A company placed "On hold" (e.g., over credit limit or delinquent) blocks new orders until resolved, but does not affect their historical order visibility.
- Quantity-break pricing is evaluated per line item by default (configurable to cart-level aggregate across variants of the same product).

## Validation
Credit limit must be ≥ 0; payment terms selection required at company creation; price list overrides must reference valid, active products; buyer email must be unique per company (can exist as a separate retail customer record independently).

## Notifications
Order pending approval (to company Admin buyer), order approved/rejected (to requesting buyer), credit limit nearing threshold (to Finance/Owner), invoice due soon / overdue (to company + internal Finance), quote responded to (to buyer).

## Audit logs
`company.created/edited/status_changed`, `credit_limit.changed`, `price_list.changed`, `order.approval_granted/rejected`, `payment_terms.changed`.

## Database schema
```
companies(id, store_id, name, tax_id, price_list_id, payment_terms, credit_limit,
          outstanding_balance, status, created_at)
company_locations(id, company_id, address, is_billing, is_shipping)
company_buyers(id, company_id, customer_id, role[admin|buyer], requires_approval,
               approval_threshold)
price_lists(id, store_id, name, currency, created_at)
price_list_entries(id, price_list_id, product_variant_id, price, min_quantity)
b2b_quotes(id, company_id, requested_by, status, line_items jsonb, quoted_total,
           expires_at, created_at)
```

## APIs
`GET/POST /api/v1/b2b/companies`, `GET/PATCH /api/v1/b2b/companies/{id}`, `POST /api/v1/b2b/companies/{id}/buyers`, `GET/POST /api/v1/b2b/price-lists`, `POST /api/v1/b2b/quotes`, `POST /api/v1/b2b/quotes/{id}/convert`, `POST /api/v1/orders/{id}/approve` (B2B approval action on the Orders resource).

## Events
`company.created`, `company.credit_limit_changed`, `company.status_changed`, `order.pending_approval`, `order.approved`, `quote.created`, `quote.converted` — consumed by Orders (Vol 2.2), Finance (Vol 2.8, AR ledger), CRM (Vol 10).

## Edge cases
Buyer belongs to no company (falls back to retail pricing, not an error state); price list with overlapping quantity-break tiers (validation prevents ambiguous tier definitions); company credit limit lowered below current outstanding balance (existing balance grandfathered, new orders blocked until paid down); quote expires before buyer accepts (auto-expire, buyer must request a new quote).

## Error handling
Net-terms order placed when credit check service is degraded → fail closed (hold for manual approval) rather than silently allowing unlimited credit exposure; price list import with invalid tier definitions → row-level error report, valid entries still applied.

## Performance
Price resolution for a logged-in buyer (correct tiered price for their company) must be as fast as standard product pricing (<20ms) — pre-resolved/cached per company, not computed by scanning all price list entries per page view.

## Security
Company/buyer data strictly scoped — one company's buyers cannot see another company's pricing, order history, or credit terms; approval-threshold bypass attempts (e.g., splitting one large order into several smaller ones to dodge approval) are flagged for merchant review, not silently allowed.

## AI opportunities
AI-suggested credit limits based on order history/payment behavior; predicted late-payment risk scoring; AI-drafted quote responses; anomaly detection on order-splitting-to-avoid-approval patterns.

## UX improvements
Company-scoped storefront view toggle for staff (see exactly what a given buyer sees); visual credit-utilization gauge; bulk quote-to-order conversion for recurring wholesale orders; reorder-from-history shortcut for repeat buyers.

## Estimated scope
~8 sub-views, ~10 API endpoints, 6 DB tables, ~35 functional requirements.
